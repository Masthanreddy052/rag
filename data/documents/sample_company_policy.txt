Company Leave Policy (Sample)

Employees receive  casual leave and sick leave according to their employment
agreement and the applicable company policy. Employees should submit leave
requests to their manager through the HR portal before taking planned leave.

For emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably
possible. Leave approval depends on the employee's available balance and
manager approval.
